# worcester_weather_comp_capstone
I am going to create 4 dashboards that compare my beautiful city of Worcester, Massachusetts and compares it's weather to what are consider to be the 3 cities in the world with the best weather, San Diego, CA, Medillin, Columbia and the Canary Island.

The first dashboard will be a real time comp of the weather. The second will compare monthly average temps. My third dashboard will show stability, how much change is there in weather in each city.  Last my fourth dashboard will show a Best Weather Score.  I am going to metric based on temperature, humidity, precipitation, wind and stability to create a score for best weather.
